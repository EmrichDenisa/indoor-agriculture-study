# indoor-agriculture-study
The objective of this research is to investigate whether machine-learning models can classify the risk of overwatering in potted plants using soil-moisture measurements and additional environmental and pot-related characteristics.

Overwatering is influenced by several factors, including soil moisture, the characteristics of the growing medium, pot dimensions, drainage properties, and watering history. Consequently, soil-moisture measurements alone may not provide sufficient information to accurately assess the watering condition of a plant.

This research formulates overwatering-risk assessment as a supervised classification problem. Given a set of input features describing the conditions of a potted plant, the model predicts the corresponding watering-risk category.
